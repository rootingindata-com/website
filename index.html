A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Is my business too small for this?

Small teams often have the most to gain from removing repetitive work. We can start with one clear problem and agree a focused project that fits your budget and priorities.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Explain what happens today, what is frustrating and what a better outcome looks like. We can explore the options together before deciding what to build.

Does everything need AI?

No. A straightforward integration or script is often the right answer. AI is an option for tasks such as document extraction or classification when it is useful and can be reviewed and tested.

Who owns the code and the data?

Code ownership, access and any third-party licences are agreed in the project scope. The aim is a documented solution you can keep using, with your business retaining control of its data.

What happens after the project?

Each project includes an agreed handover. Maintenance, monitoring and further improvements can be arranged separately, with the responsibilities made clear.
