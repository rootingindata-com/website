# Freelance automation and data consultancy: competitor and website research

Reviewed 22 November 2026. Prepared for Sylwester Stocki.

## Recommendation

Position the business as an independent software, data and automation consultancy for small businesses. Lead with practical deliverables: document extraction, automated reporting, connected systems and internal tools. Your senior software engineering and Spark/ETL background supports this offer. Advertise predictive modelling or specialist data science only where you can demonstrate the relevant experience.

Use Veriphae as the primary visual reference, Cheltenham Data for a clear solo-practitioner proposition, and Artellis for explaining a before/after workflow. These are design references, not reusable licensed templates. For an existing Jekyll template, Serif is the best fit of the two demos reviewed.

## Scope and evidence

15 businesses: eight in the Republic of Ireland and seven in the UK, including two in Northern Ireland. Selected for relevance to automation, analysis, extraction and custom software rather than size alone. Founder-led/small-team descriptions are explicit for several businesses; exact headcounts were not independently verified. Services, credentials, testimonials and results on company sites are their own claims. Inclusion is not a recommendation to hire them.

All 15 official sites were opened for content research. Six homepages were inspected in a live desktop browser, including their visible layouts, stylesheet references, script references and computed fonts. Two Jekyll demos were also inspected. This is not a mobile, accessibility, security or performance audit. Client-side files do not establish a site's complete backend stack.

## Common service categories worth considering

- Workflow automation: move information between email, spreadsheets, CRM and accounts software.
- Document extraction: convert PDFs, invoices, forms and catalogues into validated structured records.
- Reporting: consolidate data, calculate agreed KPIs and produce repeatable dashboards/reports.
- Data cleaning and matching: deduplicate, standardise, reconcile and match records.
- Web/API extraction: gather agreed data sources and maintain scheduled updates.
- Custom software: internal tools and integrations where existing products leave gaps.
- Support: monitoring, maintenance, documentation and staff handover.

For your first website, group these into four clear services: Automation; Data extraction; Reporting and analysis; Custom software and integrations. Training and fractional leadership can wait until there is demonstrated demand and capacity.

## Existing Jekyll templates

### Preferred: Serif by Zerostatic

Demo: https://jekyll-serif.netlify.app/
Source: https://github.com/zerostaticthemes/jekyll-serif-theme

- A business-oriented, multipage theme with service and team collections.
- SCSS and Bootstrap grid/utilities; documentation states GitHub Pages compatibility.
- Better structural fit for dedicated automation/extraction/reporting pages than a blog-first theme.
- Customise its red palette, illustration and default accounting content. Replace the team section with an individual About page if appropriate.
- The repository contains an MIT licence, while its README separately says not to port or resell the theme. Your intended use is an adapted business website, not theme resale; retain licence notices and clarify with the author if your use extends further.

### Alternative: Agency Jekyll Theme by Ravi Riley

Demo: https://raviriley.github.io/agency-jekyll-theme-starter/
Source: https://github.com/raviriley/agency-jekyll-theme

- Bootstrap-based, with services, portfolio, timeline, team and contact sections.
- MIT-licensed; offers a starter repository for GitHub Pages.
- Convenient for an initial one-page site, but the stock photographic hero and generic agency styling need substantial visual changes.
- Its contact form needs a configured submission service; static hosting does not process email by itself.

Recommendation: custom Jekyll layout inspired by Veriphae if distinctiveness matters most; Serif if you want a ready-made structure. Competitor websites are inspiration references, not installable Jekyll templates.

## Proposed homepage and content structure

1. Header: Services, Examples, About, Contact, plus one clear booking button.
2. Hero: outcome-oriented headline with a concrete document-to-data illustration.
3. Four service cards: automation, extraction, reporting, custom software/integrations.
4. Three example projects with source material, delivered output and honest limitations.
5. Process: short discovery, agreed scope/quote, build/review, handover/support.
6. Personal introduction: senior engineering experience, relevant data pipeline work and location.
7. FAQ: project fit, pricing approach, data handling, ownership and continuing support.
8. Final contact prompt with email and optional booking link.

Suggested original headline: “Less manual work. More useful data.”
Suggested supporting copy: “I build automation, data extraction tools and reporting systems for small businesses in Ireland and the UK.”
Suggested CTA: “Discuss your project.”

Suggested initial demonstrations:

- PDF catalogue to CSV/JSON, including validation and fields requiring review.
- Automated reporting from a sample spreadsheet and CRM export.
- Supplier/product record matching with a review queue for uncertain matches.

Label these as demonstrations if they are not paid client projects. Show genuine outputs without inventing clients, testimonials or measured savings. Distinguish experience gained in employment from independent client work.

Suggested routes: /, /services/automation/, /services/data-extraction/, /services/reporting/, /services/custom-software/, /projects/, /about/, /contact/, /privacy/.

Use Liquid includes, Markdown collections, CSS variables, Grid/Flexbox and a small amount of JavaScript for navigation or demonstrations. Render core service content in HTML. Keep forms and any future extraction application separate from the static marketing site; never put API secrets in browser JavaScript.

## GitHub Pages and hosting

Technically these layouts can be built in Jekyll and published as static files. GitHub's current Pages documentation and terms, however, restrict using Pages as free hosting to run an online business or primarily facilitate commercial transactions or commercial SaaS. The reviewed wording does not explicitly settle every freelance brochure-site case; do not assume this intended customer-acquisition site is permitted simply because it has no checkout.

Keep Jekyll and GitHub source control. For the production business website, either clarify your intended use with GitHub or select hosting appropriate for the business. Cloudflare documents deploying Jekyll from a GitHub repository, so changing the host does not require changing the framework.

- https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits
